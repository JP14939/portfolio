01 / 02
RallyMax — AI Tennis Swing Analysis

Film a swing on your phone and get a 0–100 score against professional players, a breakdown by phase of the swing, and coaching tips. I built it end to end, including the ML pipeline: MediaPipe pose, YOLO models for the ball, racket and net, an audio classifier that finds the moment of contact (89% within ±3 frames), and Dynamic Time Warping against a database of ~650 pro swings. Runs on iOS, Android and web, deployed with Docker and CI/CD. I've recently added two developers to the team to speed up development.
